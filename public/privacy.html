Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains what the Proofy waitlist at www.proofy.site collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • How you found us: the page that linked to us and any campaign tags (UTM parameters) in the link you followed.
  • A scrambled version of your IP address (a one-way hash), used only to limit repeated signups and block abuse. We don't store the IP address itself.
  • Dates: when you signed up, confirmed your email, or unsubscribed.

The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

To send you a confirmation email, tell you when Proofy launches and invite you to try it, and to understand which channels bring people to the waitlist. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the waitlist database.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Our web host serves the site and runs the signup form.

How long we keep it

Until Proofy has launched and waitlist invitations have gone out, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link that works immediately. To have your data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, reply to any email from us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
